ZingTalk Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZingTalk ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards user information across our Smart TV and mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: When you create an account, we process your display name, email address, and a randomly generated 10-digit User ID (UID) to route calls.
  • Signaling Metadata: Temporary session signaling data (SDP offers and ICE candidates) is transmitted over secure WebSockets solely to establish peer-to-peer connections.
  • Zero Media Storage: We do NOT record, store, or monitor your voice or video calls. All media streams travel directly between connected peers using WebRTC encryption.

2. Advertising & Programmatic Partners

To keep ZingTalk free, we work with authorized global advertising partners. Advertising SDKs may process non-personal device identifiers (such as Amazon Fire Advertising ID or Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and coarse network diagnostics to deliver contextual and compliant advertisements.

3. Children's Privacy & Age Rating (13+)

ZingTalk is intended for users aged 13 and older in strict compliance with COPPA regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. For data deletion requests, contact our support desk at support.zing.app@gmail.com.

4. Data Security

All platform traffic is encrypted via TLS 1.3 (HTTPS/WSS) and authenticated through industry-standard token security.

Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

By installing or using ZingTalk on Amazon Fire TV, Android TV, or mobile devices, you agree to these Terms of Service.

1. Acceptable Use

You agree to use ZingTalk strictly for lawful communication. Harassment, unauthorized automated dialing, or transmission of unlawful content will result in immediate account termination.

2. No Emergency Calls

ZingTalk is an internet-based VoIP communication tool and is not a replacement for traditional telephone networks. It does not support emergency calls to law enforcement, medical, or fire services (e.g., 112, 911, 100).

Hardware Permissions Disclosure

ZingTalk requests minimal runtime device permissions strictly required for real-time calling:

  • Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`): Accessed exclusively during active video calls to stream your camera feed.
  • Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`): Accessed exclusively during active calls to transmit audio.
  • Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used to alert you of incoming calls.
  • Network (`android.permission.INTERNET`): Required for WebRTC signaling, media relays, and account synchronization.
